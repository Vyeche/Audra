# The driver's history

This folder holds what builds and installs the `snd-usb-babyface-pro` driver. Its reverse-engineering notes, USB captures and review history are in the upstream project, [ismail-bahloul/babyface-pro-linux](https://github.com/ismail-bahloul/babyface-pro-linux), from which this copy was imported (v0.2.2) and then extended.
